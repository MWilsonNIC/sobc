About us

Special Olympics BC, Comox Valley provides sport training opportunities for children, youth, and adults with intellectual disabilities in a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community.

What is Special Olympics?

Special Olympics is a global movement that uses sport to create inclusion, confidence, and connection. Through year-round training and competition, athletes have the opportunity to build skills, improve health, experience achievement, and form lasting friendships.

Our local

In the Comox Valley, our local chapter brings athletes, families, volunteers, and supporters together through programs, events and competitions. We are proud to be part of the wider Special Olympics BC community while offering programs that reflect the needs of local athletes.

Who we serve

We serve athletes with intellectual disabilities and welcome participants with a wide range of interests, experience, ability levels, and goals. Some athletes are new to sport, while others are experienced competitors. What they share is a place to belong, grow, and have fun.

Why it matters

Special Olympics is about much more than sport. It is about confidence, friendships, health, personal growth, and community. Our programs give athletes the chance to challenge themselves, celebrate achievement progress, and be recognized for what they can do.

If you are interested in joining as an athlete, volunteering, coaching, or supporting our local, we would love to hear from you.
