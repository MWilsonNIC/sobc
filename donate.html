Support Local Athletes

Your gift helps Special Olympics BC, Comox Valley offer year-round sport opportunities for local athletes with intellectual disabilities.

Donations are processed by Special Olympics BC and directed to our Comox Valley chapter.

How Your Gift Helps

Donations support programs, equipment, facility costs, events, and the local opportunities that make participation possible for athletes and families.

When you give, you help create belonging, confidence, friendship, and joy through sport.
