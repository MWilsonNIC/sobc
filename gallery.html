Gallery

Our gallery celebrates the athletes, volunteers, families, and community members who make Special Olympics BC, Comox Valley such a welcoming and joyful place.

Photos from practices, competitions, celebrations, and events help tell the story of our local and the people who make it special.

View more photos on our Flickr account.

We are grateful for the privilege to live, work, and play on the unceded traditional territory of the K'ómoks First Nation and acknowledge the rich culture of the Pentlatch, Sathloot ('sath-loot), Sasitla (sa-'seet-la), Ieeksen (eye-'ick-sun) and Xa'xe ('ha-hey) peoples.
