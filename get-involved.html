Get Involved

We are always happy to welcome new athletes and volunteers to Special Olympics BC, Comox Valley. Use this page to find the right registration path. If you are not sure which option applies to you, please contact us and we will be glad to help.

Athlete registration

If you are interested in joining as an athlete, please complete the required registration forms. Once your forms have been submitted, our local team will follow up with more information about available programs and next steps.

Volunteers make our programs possible. Whether you are interested in coaching, helping at events, supporting athletes, or assisting behind the scenes, we would love to hear from you.

What to expect

Program availability may vary by season and local capacity. Submitting a form does not guarantee immediate placement in a specific sport, but we will do our best to help you find the right fit.
