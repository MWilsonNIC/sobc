Special Olympics BC ~ Comox Valley

Special Olympics BC, Comox Valley offers year-round sport training for athletes with intellectual disabilities in a welcoming and supportive community. Whether you are looking to join as an athlete, volunteer, coach, or supporter, we are glad you are here.

Register View Sports View Calendar

What we do

At Special Olympics BC, sport is the starting point for confidence, friendship, health, and personal growth. Our local chapter brings athletes, families, volunteers, and supporters together through programs that build skills, create connections, and celebrate achievement.

We welcome new and returning athletes, families, volunteers, and community supporters. If you are not sure where to begin, we are happy to help you find the right program or next step.

Our Comox Valley programs change over time based on season, facilities, and volunteer capacity. Visit the Sports page for current offerings, practice information, and local contact details.
